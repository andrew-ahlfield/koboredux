# Theme catalog

Everything the Kobo Redux engine asks a graphics or sound theme for, and
where the original **Halcyon** themes stand on each item. Halcyon is the
default theme pair (`GFX>>halcyon`, `SFX>>halcyon`, set in `src/config.h`);
`GFX>>mono` and `SFX>>chip` remain the fallbacks.

Status values:

- **done**: provided by Halcyon.
- **silent (by design)**: a sound slot Halcyon leaves undefined on purpose,
  because another slot already covers the moment. The engine treats every
  sound slot as optional. The `chip` theme leaves the same slots empty.
- **not used**: declared by the engine but not read by any game theme (the
  fallback `mono` theme does not provide it either).
- **kept**: provided by an existing theme that Halcyon does not replace.

All Halcyon art is generated by `tools/art/halcyon` (see
`tools/art/README.md`); the music is generated by
`tools/audio/halcyon_music.py`. Both themes are CC BY-SA 4.0.

### Region tiles and backgrounds

Five regions, each with its own directory `region-1` to `region-5` and its
own material: naval gunmetal with cyan lights, desert khaki with rust trim
and green lights, olive drab with yellow hazard paint, plum armour with
gold trim and pink lights, white ceramic with red markings. Bases are drawn
as armoured installations: plated corridors with a south wall and a drop
shadow, hubs with bunkers, domes, towers and landing pads, twin barrel
turrets, reactor cores. Tile sheets are 8x7 frames: 0 is the alternate 4
way junction (landing pad); 1 to 15 are indexed by connection mask (up 1,
right 2, down 4, left 8; single bits are cannon turrets); 16 to 23 animate
the core vertically and 24 to 31 horizontally; 32 to 35 are hard nodes
(up, right, down, left); 40 to 45 are corridor variants (blast door, vent
block, pipe bundle; even frames vertical); 48 to 55 are wreckage stubs.
The 12 and 8 px sets are box filtered from the 16 px set.

## Sound

Programs live in `data/sfx/halcyon`: `master.a2s` (mixer groups),
`sfx.a2s` (game effects), `uisfx.a2s` (user interface), `music.a2s`
(generated). Effects take `(pitch volume pan)`; continuous effects also
handle `1()` stop, `2(Dmg)` damage and `3(npP npV npX Dur)` position.

## What is left

Nothing the engine reads is missing from either Halcyon theme. Possible
future work, none of it required:

- Distinct sprites for `B_ROCK2` and `B_ROCK3` (currently aliases of
  `B_ROCK1`, which `mono` also does).
- Separate particle definitions for each enemy kind (now aliases of
  `PFX_ENEMY1`).
- Longer arrangements for the music: each track is one eight bar
  progression arranged in three to four sections.
